The Divide is two things at once: a treaty and a river. As a treaty it ended the War of Residue in 712 A.T., the year a second AonDor blast forced mortals and elves to stop. As a river it marks the eastern edge of [[Locales/Astravia/Avryn/Avryn\|Avryn]], and beyond it lie the elvenlands of [[Erivan\|Erivan]]. Most Astravians know only the pact. Avrysians also know the river, and know that it is not to be crossed. 

## Why It Was Signed
When a second AonDor was found near the northern cliffs, Delgos and Erivan converged on it, and the history of that time is murky, with Delgosian and Nyphron sources contradicting each other. One truth survives: the elven forces were far superior, with a mastery of the arcane that was ruthless and indifferent to death, and an ageless vitality that no sorcerer or paladin could put out.

The clerics of [[Nyphron\|Nyphron]], their god gone, turned their magic into an abomination to match the immortals: the [[The Library/Gilarabrywn\|Gilarabrywn]]. Delgos retreated the moment the first beasts reached the battlefield, leaving the scarred Holy Empire to face the elves alone. The war took its name from the strange residue those god-defying spells left behind, and it raged for centuries, with the Gilarabrywn outliving their makers and laying waste to the north.

Then the AonDor was "opened" once more, for reasons nobody can explain, and the blast killed countless people on both sides and ended the war in 712 A.T. 

## The Pact
Both sides came away believing the AonDor was never a prize, only a cruel test of how quickly creation could destroy itself. The terms followed from that. 
* Mortalkind never crosses the river. To cross it is an act of war against the elves, and for nearly seven centuries nobody has dared.
* The last known AonDor, in the mountains of Erivan, is never to be activated.
* The Holy Empire gives up the knowledge of the Gilarabrywn, and with it the hunger for power that made them.

Nobody has ever confirmed the AonDor is still there. The fear of a second war against beings who seem unkillable has kept every explorer on the western bank. The elves are said to remember the first and second AonDors personally, and to challenge those memories is to challenge the arcane power of all elvenkind. 

## What It Changed
* The Empire split into [[Locales/Astravia/Trent/Trent\|Trent]], a coalition of hardened survivors sworn to defend the north, and Avryn, a pale shadow of its former glory.
* [[Locales/Astravia/Avryn/Ghent/Ervanon\|Ervanon]] collapsed under the weight of its failure and the capital moved to Melengar, the start of what the PDF calls a humble, bitter new age.
* [[Locales/Astravia/Trent/Lingard\|Lingard]] was born from the same wreckage. Its people guard the river from the Silent Watch towers, and their true loyalty is to the Pact of the Divide, not to the crown in Lanksteer.

## The Gilarabrywn Connection
The Gilarabrywn belong to the same years as the pact: they were made during the War of Residue, and surrendering their making was part of the price of ending it. Their method is recorded in [[The Library/Gilarabrywn\|Gilarabrywn]].
