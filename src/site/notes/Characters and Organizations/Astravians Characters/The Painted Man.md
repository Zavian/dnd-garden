> #orange The Merchant of Mysteries
> "The Painted Man does not deal in gold, not truly. He deals in the things gold cannot buy: a memory returned, a debt of blood settled, a secret that can topple a Trade Prince. Just be certain you are willing to pay what he asks when the time comes."

Tall, stout, and smelling of rare spices, the **Painted Man** is a fixture of [[Locales/Astravia/Avryn/Warric/Colnora\|Colnora]]'s magical world. His true name remains a secret, hidden behind the elaborate mask that he wears all the time. He operates **The Black Lotus**, a curiosity shop in Lower Colnora.

He requested from the group the recovery of the **Weltzman box**, a powerful artifact. The group knows that it was stolen by [[Characters and Organizations/Astravians Characters/Sergeant Hendryk\|Sergeant Hendryk]]
