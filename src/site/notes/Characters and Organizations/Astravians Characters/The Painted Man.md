> #orange The Merchant of Mysteries
> "The Painted Man does not deal in gold, not truly. He deals in the things gold cannot buy: a memory returned, a debt of blood settled, a secret that can topple a Trade Prince. Just be certain you are willing to pay what he asks when the time comes."

Tall, stout, and smelling of rare spices, the **Painted Man** is a fixture of [[Locales/Astravia/Avryn/Warric/Colnora\|Colnora]]'s magical world. His true name remains a secret, hidden behind the elaborate mask that he wears all the time. He operates **The Black Lotus**, a curiosity shop in Lower Colnora.

He requested from the group the recovery of the **Weltzman box**, a powerful artifact. The group knows that it was stolen by [[Characters and Organizations/Astravians Characters/Sergeant Hendryk\|Sergeant Hendryk]]

::hidden

He is the ficture of colnora's underworld and uses his shop as a front for his extensive magical services.

## The Merchant of Shadow
It is an open secret that if you need magical assistance—from the arcane to the forbidden—you go to the Painted Man. He always helps, for a price. While mundane magic might require coin, more potent requests demand outlandish costs: the recovery of a specific relic from the [[Locales/Otherwordly/Ethereal Plane/Aether Expanse\|Aether Expanse]], the delivery of a potion into a specific guard's goblet, or a favor to be named at a later date.

He never asks for payment immediately, but he *always* collects. No one in Colnora is known to have successfully denied his requests.

## Origin & Rumors
The Painted Man's origins are shrouded in mystery. Some claim he has lived in Colnora for decades, an exiled member of a cabal of arcanists from a distant, eastern land. Others whisper that he is not even human, but a creature of the Void wearing a fat man's skin. 

Regardless of his past, his power is undeniable. His network of favors allows him access to skills far outside his own purview, and his trade in curios has placed powerful artifacts into his possession.

## The Black Lotus
His shop, located near the river, appears to be a nondescript curios shop. Inside, however, lies a library of forbidden knowledge, an alchemical lab, and a hidden basement containing a summoning circle stained with old blood.

* **The Vault:** He is known to have an interest in the deeper secrets of the world, often sending adventurers to retrieve items of historical or magical significance.
