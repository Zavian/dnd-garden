> Eccentric merchant from [[Locales/Astravia/Calis/Gur Em/Dagastan\|Dagastan]]. His allegiance is unknown other than someone known as **White Cane**
> First meeting: [[Session Journals/Session 13 - 28th June\|Session 13 - 28th June]]

His first appearance happened during the auction of Hunclay's manor in [[Locales/Astravia/Avryn/Warric/Belhaim\|Belhaim]]. He did not purchase anything and just observed the auction itself.
He was first mentioned by Morc and Fren who knew him from a previous auction in [[Locales/Astravia/Delgos/Maranon/Manzar (City)\|Manzar (City)]] where he purchased a powerful artifact that would allow the tracing of beings who had been in contact with other planes.
