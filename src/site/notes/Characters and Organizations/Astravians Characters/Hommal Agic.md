**Hommal Agic** is the owner of a five-story garden tenement on the waterfront of [[Locales/Astravia/Avryn/Warric/Colnora\|Colnora]]. An honest businessman by nature, he has inadvertently become one of the most important neutral figures in the city's underworld.

## The Gardener of the Docks
Hommal's true passion is his **Botanical Rooftop**, a lush and overgrown garden of greenhouses, patios, and rare flora. He maintains a small monopoly on certain fibers and extracts used by brewers and alchemists across Avryn.

* **The Neutral Ground:** Because he stays silent and serves excellent tea, his rooftop has been accepted as a neutral meeting place for the city's rival criminal organizations. He survives by being useful to everyone and threatening to no one.
* **The Alchemical Supplier:** He supplies the ingredients for many of the city's "medicines," though he has recently been strong-armed into including more addictive substances in his shipments to certain "shadow" potion shops.
