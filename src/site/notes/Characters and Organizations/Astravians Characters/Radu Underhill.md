> #orange The Discreet Liaison
> "If you need a body to disappear, a secret to stay buried, or a conversation with the things that walk the deeper dark... you find Radu. He is polite, reliable, and entirely without a soul. A true gentleman of the underworld."

In life, **Radu Underhill** was a broad-shouldered gentleman of means, the last scion of the old **[[Characters and Organizations/Noble Houses/Colnora/House Underhill\|House Underhill]]**. In death, he has kept himself in surprisingly excellent condition. As a ghou, he can still pass for human in the proper light, maintaining the coiffed and groomed appearance of the noble he once was.
