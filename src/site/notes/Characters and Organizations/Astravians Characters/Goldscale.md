> #orange The Golden Spark
> "In a city of shadows, even a small light can be blinding. They call him a fool, a lizard playing at being a knight. But when the Black Diamond comes for the weak in the Ghetto, they find that his scales aren't just for show."

Set apart from most of his kobold kin by the faint golden sheen of his scaly hide, **Goldscale** exudes a strength springing from an unflagging faith and a iron-clad sense of justice. In the dark, damp tunnels of [[Locales/Astravia/Avryn/Warric/Colnora\|Colnora]]'s **Kobold Ghetto**, he is a shining example that righteousness can exist in the unlikeliest of places.

## The Paladin of the Ghetto
Goldscale developed his signature golden scales while quite young. His father attempted to use his appearance to claim draconic heritage and seize leadership of the Ghetto through blood and violence. Traumatized by the ensuing chaos, Goldscale swore to never again allow the criminals of Colnora's streets to trample the innocent. 

He pledged himself to **[[Gods and Pantheons/Astravia/Apsu\|Apsu]]**, being one of the very few people that are even seen with her symbols and tenets. He patrols the city—recognizing no boundaries between the surface and the Cartways—looking to right injustices and protect those the Merchant Council has forgotten.
