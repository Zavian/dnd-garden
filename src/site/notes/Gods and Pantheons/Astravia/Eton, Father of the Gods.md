Eton is the father of the Gods, and representative of the Sky. He is the husband of [[Elan\|Elan]] 

Unlike his children the Aesira, Eton is truly immortal and eternal. He was responsible for imprisoning the Typhons in Phyre for their destructive habits, and punished Turin for waging war on his siblings by killing Alurya. Attempts by Turin to kill him following his punishment of Alurya ended in failure.
