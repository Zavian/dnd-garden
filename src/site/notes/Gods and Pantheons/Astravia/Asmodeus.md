The **Prince of Darkness** and the **Dark Whisper** of the [[Locales/Otherwordly/The Underscape/The Underscape\|Underscape]]. While technically considered a part of the **Nyphron Covenant** due to the historical influence of **House Thrune**, Asmodeus is not a god in the traditional sense. He is an Archdevil, a being of immense power who trades in contracts, pride, and the tyranny of the strong over the weak.
