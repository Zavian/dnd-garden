**Nocticula** is the enigma of the [[Gods and Pantheons/Astravia/The Prismatic Ray\|Prismatic Ray]]. Unlike her peers, she was not born of the celestial lights but ascended from the shadows to join them. Once the Demon Lord of Assassins and Lust, she is the only known entity to have fully "completed" herself within the [[Locales/Otherwordly/The Underscape/The Underscape\|Underscape]] and transcended it.

She represents the **Midnight**—not as a time of fear, but as a threshold of transition. She is the proof that one's nature is not carved in stone.

# History
Her story is one of the most debated theological topics in Astravia. As a Demon Lord, she ruled the Midnight Isles in the [[Locales/Otherwordly/The Underscape/The Underscape#The Endless Hunger (Abyss)\|Abyss]], a realm of seduction and death. Yet, over eons, she grew weary of the static nature of evil. She realized that the "hunger" of the Abyss was a trap, not a power.

Through acts of subtle subversion and the slow rejection of her own demonic nature, she forged a new path. She did not seek forgiveness from the gods; she simply *became* something else. Her ascension shattered a portion of the Abyss, leaving behind a scar that the other Demon Lords still fear.

Now, she stands as the **Redeemer Queen**, a patron to those the world has thrown away.

# The Church of the Second Chance
Her temples are often found in the "wrong" parts of town—in slums, near prisons, or in the back alleys of artist districts. They are not grand cathedrals, but safehouses.
* **The Midnight Mass:** Her services are held at night, often involving the sharing of secrets and the symbolic burning of one's past mistakes.
* **The Exiles:** Her clergy are almost entirely made up of people who have "started over": former thieves, disgraced nobles, and even monstrous races who seek a life beyond their instincts.
