Elan is the mother of the gods, and is a representation of the Earth.

She is the wife of [[Gods and Pantheons/Astravia/Eton, Father of the Gods\|Eton]], 

Artists often hear the voice of Elan guiding them. It's often described as not an actual voice, but thoughts or ideas that seem different from what a person would normally think, or as the same type of instinct that causes, for example, squirrels to gather nuts for winter. However, it is often sometimes quite cryptic.
