While the **Prismatic Ray** serves as the spiritual bedrock for much of Astravia—uniting the Sun, Moon, Songbird, Dove, and Night Star into a singular, harmonious pantheon—the fractured nations have naturally gravitated toward "Faint Divinities" and the "Aesira Children" who echo their specific struggles.

---

## 0. The Prismatic Ray (The Aesira)
*The High Gods of the Mantle.*
These five deities are the architects of the **Sanctum's Mantle**. They are rarely petitioned for mundane tasks, instead representing the cosmic balance of the Rhythm.

## 1. The Nyphron Covenants (Avryn)
*The Gods of Civilization and the Legacy of Men.*
The Nyphron Church centers its worship on the "Third Son," Maribor, while respecting the legalistic frameworks of his subordinates.

| **Deity**                            | **Title**           | **Domains**                 | **Symbol**            | **Portfolio**                                                                                                           |
| :----------------------------------- | :------------------ | :-------------------------- | :-------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **[[Gods and Pantheons/Astravia/God of Men, Maribor\|Maribor]]** | The God of Men      | Humanity, Survival, Legacy  | A stylized human hand | The patron of all human civilization. He represents the "Golden Age" of Nyphron and the resilience of the human spirit. |
| **[[Gods and Pantheons/Astravia/Abadar\|Abadar]]**                       | Master of the Vault | Law, Cities, Wealth         | A golden key          | Civilization, commerce, and architecture. He is the hand that writes the laws of the Noble Houses.                      |
| **[[Gods and Pantheons/Astravia/Irori\|Irori]]**                        | Master of Masters   | Self-Perfection, Discipline | An open blue palm     | Self-improvement through struggle. Worshipped by the martial scholars of Rhenydd.                                       |
| **[[Gods and Pantheons/Astravia/Asmodeus\|Asmodeus]]**                     | The Dark Whisper    | Tyranny, Contracts, Fire    | A red pentagram       | Pride and forbidden pacts. The shadow that whispers to the remnants of House Thrune.                                    |

*The Gods of Paradox, Forge, and Innovation.*

| **Deity**                      | **Title**             | **Domains**            | **Symbol**                    | **Portfolio**                                                                                                 |
| :----------------------------- | :-------------------- | :--------------------- | :---------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **[[Gods and Pantheons/Astravia/Drome\|Drome]]**                  | God of Dwarves        | Forge, Earth, Industry | A hammer over a mountain      | The father of industry and the creator of the first "Raysors." Patron of smiths and the Clank race.           |
| **[[Gods and Pantheons/Astravia/Nethys\|Nethys]]**                 | The All-Seeing        | Magic, Duality         | A mask, half white/half black | Raw arcane power without morality. The guiding light of the League of Miracles.                               |
| **[[Gods and Pantheons/Astravia/Brigh\|Brigh]]**                  | The Whisper in Bronze | Clockwork, Invention   | A gear-faced mask             | The goddess of machines and sentient constructs. Worshiped by many constructs hailing from Alkenstar.         |

| :----------------------------- | :----------------- | :---------------------- | :----------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |

| **[[Gods and Pantheons/Astravia/Gozreh\|Gozreh]]**                 | The Storm-Born     | Weather, Sea, Air       | A leaf dripping water    | The indifferent fury of the elements. Petitioned by sailors and those braving the volcanic Sound.             |
| **[[Gods and Pantheons/Astravia/Gorum\|Gorum]]**                  | Our Lord in Iron   | War, Battle, Strength   | A sword in a mountain    | The patron of survival through violence. Worshiped by the exiles and barbarians of the tundra.                |

## 4. The Seldarine (Erivan)
*The Ancient Gods of Elvenkind.*
To the Elves, the gods are not distant creators, but ancestors who left the AonDor as a legacy.

| **Deity**    | **Title**    | **Domains**                 | **Symbol**                | **Portfolio**                                                                                            |
| :----------- | :----------- | :-------------------------- | :------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ferrol**   | God of Elves | Elvenkind, Vitality, Arcane | A silver willow tree      | The eldest son and patron of the immortal elven soul. Represents the ageless nature of the Erivan lands. |
| **Yuelral**  | The Wise     | Crystals, Magic, Jewels     | Three floating crystals   | The patron of the gem-magic that powers the elven cities and shields the Divide.                         |
| **Ketephys** | The Hunter   | Moon, Forestry, Borders     | A hawk on a crescent moon | The protector of the Divide and the silent warden of the elven borders.                                  |

## 5. The Shadows of AonDor (The Forgotten)
*The Entities of the Void.*
These are the forces that existed before the Mantle, or were cast out during its creation.

| **Entity** | **Title** | **Domains** | **Portfolio** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Uberlin** | God of Darkness | Shadow, Secrets, Loss | A sun eclipsed by a black circle | The son who turned away from the Light. He is the source of the "Deep Rot" and the master of the shadows beneath the earth. |
| **Yog-Sothoth** | The Key and the Gate | Void, Space, Time | The AonDor itself. The terrifying bridge between our reality and the primordial chaos beyond the Mantle. |
| **Nyarlathotep** | The Crawling Chaos | Madness, Deception | The messenger of the Forgotten Gods. The voice that goaded the Cenzylors into their hubris at Percepliquis. |
